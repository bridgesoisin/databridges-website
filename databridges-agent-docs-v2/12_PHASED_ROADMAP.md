# Outcome-Gated Roadmap

## Planning rule

Milestones are gates, not a promise to build every listed feature. Do not begin a later milestone because the calendar advanced; begin it when the previous decision evidence is sufficient.

Dates and numeric targets must be set by the named owner after capacity and current baselines are known.

## Milestone 0: activate and stabilise

### Outcome

The repository has one trusted instruction set, controlled evidence and no unowned critical trust defect.

### Required work

- approve or amend this documentation pack;
- reconcile root agent instructions and actual deployment workflow;
- decide where sensitive evidence may be stored;
- supply or mark missing evidence sources unavailable;
- audit public claims, forms, consent text, CTAs and routes;
- define current validation and CI gaps.

### Exit evidence

- pack owner and approval recorded;
- sensitive evidence location approved;
- critical claim/content defects resolved or explicitly blocked;
- active branch/deployment process matches documentation;
- validation commands are accurate.

## Milestone 1: validate the proposition

### Outcome

One primary audience, buyer problem and entry offer are supported by evidence rather than preference.

### Required work

- analyze existing enquiries, delivery and traffic where available;
- conduct targeted customer/buyer conversations if evidence is weak;
- define offer scope, owner, delivery constraints and commercial assumptions;
- establish conversion and lead-quality baselines;
- approve route and redirect plan.

### Exit evidence

- documented audience/problem/offer decision;
- evidence and rejected alternatives;
- approved success measures and stop/revisit trigger;
- route migration plan.

If the proposition remains unclear, continue research or narrow the offer. Do not compensate by adding more pages.

## Milestone 2: core consultancy experience

### Outcome

A suitable visitor can understand the offer, assess credible proof and complete a reliable next step.

### Required work

- focused homepage;
- approved service page(s);
- first publication-ready case study;
- evidence-led About refinement;
- accessible, reliable contact flow;
- one optional resource pilot.

### Exit evidence

- moderated review or equivalent evidence that target users understand audience, offer and next step;
- all public claims trace to accessible evidence;
- critical routes pass the current release checklist;
- form delivery and failure handling verified;
- baseline conversion is measurable.

## Milestone 3: improve measurement and operations

### Outcome

DataBridges can distinguish qualified demand from vanity traffic and operate follow-up reliably.

### Required work

- minimal privacy-safe analytics contract;
- lead-source and lifecycle discipline;
- explicit form/CRM architecture decision;
- CRM or email automation only where demonstrated volume/workflow needs it;
- iterative CTA, content and form improvements.

### Exit evidence

- reliable submission and delivery rate;
- qualified conversations attributable at a proportionate level;
- owner can explain funnel limitations;
- operational failures are visible;
- implementation cost is proportionate to lead volume.

Optimization begins here and continues in every milestone; it is not deferred to the end of the programme.

## Milestone 4: optional product experiments

### Visibility Index

Proceed from methodology to owned-site prototype to private pilot. Public profiles require a separate approval gate under `07_VISIBILITY_INDEX.md`.

### AI Tools Directory

Proceed from a 20–30-record manual editorial pilot. Importing a large database requires evidence of user value and sustainable maintenance under `08_AI_TOOLS_DIRECTORY.md`.

### Exit decision

For each experiment choose one:

- stop and archive learning;
- keep as an internal delivery tool;
- continue private testing;
- approve a bounded public release.

“Built successfully” is not evidence that a product should be public.

## Portfolio constraints

- One high-risk experiment at a time.
- Core client delivery and site reliability take precedence.
- Every product has an owner, support burden and stop condition.
- Accessibility, security, claims and privacy are part of each ticket, not a terminal QA phase.
- Do not start custom platform work while basic offer/conversion evidence is unresolved.

About Nore Valley Plumbing

Nore Valley Plumbing was started in 1998 by a plumber who had trained on building sites around Kilkenny and wanted to run a firm where customers could always reach the person who did the work. The company is still family owned.

Our plumbers

Our six plumbers and heating engineers are all registered, and two of them are qualified to install heat pumps. New staff spend their first year working alongside an experienced plumber before they visit customers alone.

Our standards

We keep our vans stocked, our prices written down and our promises small enough to keep. If a job runs over the quoted price because of something we missed, we absorb the cost.

Our area

We work within twenty miles of Kilkenny city, including Callan, Thomastown and Bennettsbridge. Beyond that distance we are glad to recommend a plumber we trust.

Our vehicles

Each of our vans carries a full set of common spares, a pipe freezing kit and a pressure tester. That stock means most repairs are finished on the first visit rather than the second.

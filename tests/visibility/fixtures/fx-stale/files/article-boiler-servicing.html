A practical guide to boiler servicing

By Niall Dunne, 14 March 2024

A boiler is the hardest working appliance in most homes, yet it is often ignored until it fails. A regular service is the cheapest way to avoid a cold house in January.

Why should a boiler be serviced every year?

A service keeps the burner clean, the flue clear and the controls working as the maker intended. It also reduces fuel use, and the SEAI publishes advice on running a heating system efficiently.

What does a service include?

The engineer inspects the burner, flue, pump and pressure, tests the safety devices and cleans the main components. You receive a written record of what was checked and anything that needs attention.

How long does it take?

A typical domestic service takes between forty five minutes and one hour. Older boilers or those with a long history of faults can take longer, and we will tell you before we start.

When should you call an engineer straight away?

Call immediately if you smell gas, see black marks around the boiler or hear loud banging. Turn the boiler off first, and never try to repair a gas appliance yourself.

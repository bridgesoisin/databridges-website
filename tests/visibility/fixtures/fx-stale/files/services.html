Plumbing and heating services

We cover every stage of a domestic heating job, from the first survey to the final commissioning visit. Our plumbers are registered, insured and trained on the main makes of boiler and heat pump.

Boiler servicing and repairs

An annual service keeps your boiler safe and efficient, and most repairs can be completed on the first visit. We carry the common spare parts in our vans, so you are not left without hot water.

Emergency call-outs

Burst pipes, failed boilers and leaks do not wait for office hours, so we run an out of hours service. A plumber will usually reach you within two hours anywhere in Kilkenny city.

Bathroom installations

We fit complete bathrooms, including tiling, electric shower connections and underfloor heating. A fixed price is agreed after a survey, and we coordinate any other trades that the job needs.

Heat pump upgrades

We survey your home, size the system and install air to water heat pumps. Information on the available grants is published by SEAI, and we can help with the application.

Plumbing and heating in Kilkenny

Nore Valley Plumbing is a local firm at 3 Castle Road, Kilkenny, providing boiler servicing and heat pump installation for homes and small businesses. Our registered plumbers cover Kilkenny city and the villages around it.

What we do

We service and replace boilers, fit bathrooms, repair leaks and install heat pumps. Each job is carried out by a named plumber who explains the work before it starts and tidies up properly afterwards.

Why customers call us

Most of our work comes from recommendation, and many customers have used us for more than ten years. We answer the phone in person, arrive when we say we will and give a written price in advance.

From the blog

We write occasional notes on looking after a heating system. Start with our practical guide to boiler servicing or read about choosing between a heat pump and a boiler before you replace yours.

Visit or call

Our yard and office are open on weekdays from eight until five. Call before you visit so that we can have the right parts and the right plumber ready for you.

Project gallery

First published 2 June 2025. Last updated 14 September 2026.

These are some of the jobs our plumbers have finished recently around Kilkenny. Each entry gives a short note on what we did and why.

Boiler replacements

We replaced a twenty year old oil boiler in Callan with a modern condensing unit. The new boiler cut the household heating bill by about a fifth in the first winter.

Bathroom refits

A cramped family bathroom in Thomastown became a wet room with a level access shower. We moved the waste pipes and installed heated flooring over two weeks.

Heat pump installations

A new house near Bennettsbridge now heats itself with an air to water heat pump. The system was sized to the insulation levels and commissioned with the owners present.

Commercial heating

We upgraded the boilers and controls in a small hotel kitchen without closing for a single service. Out of hours working kept the dining room open throughout the project, and the SEAI grant covered part of the cost.

How do we choose each system?

We begin with a heat loss survey, then compare running costs, grants and the space available. The recommendation we give is the one we would choose for our own homes.

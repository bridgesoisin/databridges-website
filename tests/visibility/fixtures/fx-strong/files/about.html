About Harbour Light Consulting

Harbour Light Consulting is a small advisory firm based on Quay Street in Dundalk. We help owners and managers decide where AI and automation can safely save time, and where it should be left alone.

Our story

The firm began when our founder spent a winter automating the paperwork in her family business. The results were so useful that neighbouring firms asked for the same help. She built a small team and a repeatable method, and the practice has grown steadily since.

We have worked with accountants, builders, care providers, retailers and public bodies. The details differ, but the problem is always the same: skilled people spending hours on tasks that a clear rule could handle.

How we work

We start by listening. Before we suggest any tool we spend time with the people who do the work, because they know where the real delays hide. Only then do we propose a small, low-risk first step.

Every recommendation comes with a plain explanation, a fixed price and a note of what could go wrong. We would rather lose a sale than sell something that does not fit.

The people

Our team includes a systems specialist, a trainer and a data protection adviser. Between them they have more than forty years of experience in Irish businesses. Everyone answers the telephone, and everyone has done the day job that we now help to improve.

Our trainer has taught hundreds of staff to use new software without fear. Our data protection adviser reviews every project plan before work starts, so that privacy is designed in from the beginning.

Our promise

We automate the boring tasks and protect the human parts of your work. We tell you honestly what AI can do, we never overstate results, and we leave you with the documents and the know-how to carry on without us.

If a project is not working, we will say so early and suggest a cheaper alternative. You can ask any of our past clients for a reference, and we will introduce you without delay.

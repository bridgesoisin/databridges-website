Practical AI and automation advice for Irish small businesses

Harbour Light Consulting helps small businesses in Dundalk, County Louth and across Ireland use AI and automation in a sensible, safe way. We start with an AI readiness review and finish with working systems that your team understands and trusts.

Our work covers SharePoint automation, workflow automation and plain-language training for staff. We focus on the repetitive tasks that eat into your week, such as chasing approvals, re-keying invoices and filing documents. We leave the human parts of your business, like talking to customers, firmly with your people.

What do we do for small firms?

We map how work really moves through your business, then automate the steps that follow a clear rule. Every project begins with a fixed-price review so that you know the cost before any building starts. You keep the documentation, the access and every decision.

How does an AI readiness review work?

We spend half a day with your team, look at your files, forms and inboxes, and score each process for risk and benefit. You then receive a short written report with three recommended first steps. Most clients start with one small, low-risk automation and build from there.

The report is written for owners, not engineers. It uses plain language, short sentences and a one-page summary that you can share with your accountant or your board.

Why do clients trust us?

We explain what we are doing in plain language and we never promise results that we cannot measure. Clients tell us they value our honesty about what AI cannot do. We also follow the EU rules on transparency and data protection from the first day of every project.

Our clients include accountants, builders, care providers and retailers. Many of them have been with us for several years, and they tell us that quiet, steady improvements matter more to them than any dramatic launch.

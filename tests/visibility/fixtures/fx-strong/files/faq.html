Frequently asked questions

These are the questions that small business owners ask us most often. If yours is not here, send us an email and we will answer it. We review this page every quarter, so the answers reflect how we actually work.

Before your first call it helps to have these to hand:

  • A list of the three tasks that take most of your week
  • The names of the people who carry them out
  • Any software you already pay for

Who do you work with?

We work with small and medium businesses, charities and public bodies across Ireland. Most clients have between five and fifty staff and no in-house technology team.

How much does an AI readiness review cost?

The review is a fixed-price half-day workshop, and we quote it in writing before we begin. The price depends on the size of your team, and we explain exactly what it includes.

Do you use AI on our confidential data?

We only use tools that your own organisation has approved, and we never paste customer data into public services. Every project begins with a short data handling agreement.

How long does a typical project take?

A readiness review takes one day plus a week for the report. A first automation usually takes two to four weeks, depending on how many people need to approve it.

Will automation replace my staff?

No, our aim is to remove repetitive tasks so that your people can spend more time on work that needs judgement and care. We leave the human parts of your business with your team.

Do you follow Irish and EU rules?

Yes, we follow data protection law and the transparency duties in the EU AI Act. The Data Protection Commission publishes guidance for small organisations that we recommend reading.

How do I get started?

Send us a short email describing the task that takes up most of your week. We will reply within two working days with a suggested next step.

Still unsure? Our team is happy to talk through your situation by telephone, with no obligation and no sales pitch.

Our AI and automation services

Harbour Light Consulting offers four services for small businesses in Dundalk and across Ireland. Each one is fixed-price, documented and designed so that your own team can run it afterwards.

What does an AI readiness review include?

The review is a half-day workshop followed by a short written report. We score each of your processes for risk and benefit, then recommend three first steps.

  • A map of how work flows through your business
  • A risk and benefit score for each process
  • Three recommended first steps in plain language
  • A fixed quote for any follow-on work

SharePoint automation

We build approval flows, document libraries and reminders inside the Microsoft tools you already pay for. Nothing new needs to be installed, and your staff keep working in familiar screens.

Typical projects include holiday requests, purchase approvals and contract renewals. Each flow is tested with your team before it goes live, and we write a one-page guide for every one.

Workflow automation

We connect your forms, inboxes and spreadsheets so that repetitive tasks run themselves. A person always reviews anything that touches a customer or a payment.

The aim is to remove copying and pasting, not judgement. If a step needs a human decision, the system pauses and asks for one.

Staff training in plain language

Our training sessions explain what AI can and cannot do, using examples from your own business. Staff leave with simple rules for safe use and a short checklist to keep.

Sessions run for two hours in your office or online. We never ask anyone to paste confidential material into a public tool.

Which tasks do we never automate?

We leave conversations with customers, hiring decisions and anything that needs empathy firmly with your people. Automation should free up time for those human tasks, not replace them.

How do we price our work?

Every project starts with a fixed price agreed in writing, so there are no surprises. Before you decide, you can read the overview of the EU AI Act to see which rules apply to you.

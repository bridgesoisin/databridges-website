Bespoke kitchens and wardrobes made to measure

Fernhill Joinery designs and builds bespoke kitchens for families who want a room that works as hard as they do. Every cupboard, drawer and worktop is planned around the way you cook and store food. Nothing is bought off the shelf and altered to fit.

We also make fitted wardrobes, alcove units and home offices. Our joiners work in solid oak, ash and painted timber, and they finish every piece by hand before it leaves the workshop. You will meet the person who builds your furniture long before the first board is cut. We take on only a handful of kitchens each season so that nothing is ever rushed.

How a project starts

Each job begins with a visit to your home, where we measure the room and listen to what you need. A week later you receive a drawing, a list of materials and a fixed price. Once you approve the plan we book a date for the build.

Why people choose us

Customers tell us they like our straight answers and our tidy fitters. We keep our promises on dates and we never leave a job half finished. Many of our kitchens have been in daily use for twenty years.

Where we work

Our workshop is on the edge of Galway and we travel to homes across the west of Ireland. Call in to see finished pieces, or see how our pricing works and read our guide to choosing a kitchen joiner. You can also read about our fitted wardrobes or why we choose oak or ash.

Frequently asked questions

These are the questions we hear most often. If yours is not here, please ask us.

How much does a bespoke kitchen cost?

Prices depend on the size of the room and the timber you choose, and we give a fixed quote after the first visit. Most families spend between a third and a half more than on a standard range.

How long does a project take?

From the signed drawing to the finished kitchen takes about eight weeks, while wardrobes and alcove units are quicker. We give you a firm date before you pay a deposit.

Do you work outside Galway?

Yes, we travel to homes across the west of Ireland, and longer journeys add a small delivery charge. The charge is always agreed in the quote.

Can you match my existing furniture?

We can match most timbers and finishes by testing samples in your own light. A perfect match is rare, so we will show you the closest options and you decide which you prefer.

What happens if something goes wrong?

Call us and we will visit within a week. Every piece has a ten year guarantee, and the consumer rights guide explains your wider protections.

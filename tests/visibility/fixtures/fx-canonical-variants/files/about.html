About Fernhill Joinery

Fernhill Joinery began as a one man workshop and has grown into a team of seven. We are still a family business and we still sweep the floor ourselves at the end of every day.

Our story

Our founder trained as a cabinet maker and opened the workshop after fitting his own kitchen. Neighbours asked for the same. Word spread, and the work has not stopped since.

The workshop

The workshop has a dust extraction system, a modern panel saw and a large spray booth. The machines save time on the heavy cutting. Every joint is still cut and checked by hand.

The team

Our joiners have each served a full apprenticeship, and two of them now train apprentices of their own. Our fitters have more than fifty years of experience between them. Two office staff look after quotes, orders and the diary.

Our values

We say what we will do and we do it. We quote a fixed price. We tidy up after ourselves, and we answer the telephone when you ring.

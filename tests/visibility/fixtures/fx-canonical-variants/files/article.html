How to choose a kitchen joiner

By Niamh Costello, 21 July 2026

A new kitchen is a large purchase, and the joiner you pick matters as much as the design. These five questions will help you compare the people who quote for the work.

Can I see finished kitchens?

Ask to see recent work, in person if possible, because a good joiner will gladly show you kitchens that are several years old. Look at the corners, the drawer runners and the way the doors line up.

What is included in the price?

A fixed price should cover design, materials, fitting and clearing away, and you should ask for it in writing. Check whether worktops, handles and lighting are included or extra.

How long will the work take?

Ask for a start date and a finish date, and be wary of vague answers. A busy workshop will often have a waiting list of several weeks.

Is the work guaranteed?

Look for a written guarantee of at least five years, and ask who will return if a door drops or a drawer sticks. The contractor checklist lists further points to cover.

Who will be in my house?

Find out whether the people who visit you are the people who do the work, since small teams usually give better continuity. Ask for the names of the fitters.

Our joinery services

We design, build and fit bespoke joinery for the home. Each service below is handled by the same small team from the first visit to the last coat of oil.

What do we build?

We build kitchens, wardrobes, alcove units, home offices and staircases, and every piece is made to measure in our own workshop. We do not sell flat-pack furniture.

How does a kitchen project work?

We visit your home, agree a design and give you a fixed price in writing. The build takes about six weeks, and our fitters then install everything in two or three days.

Fitted wardrobes and alcove units

Wardrobes and alcove units use the same timber and the same joints as our kitchens, and we measure every wall twice. The doors and drawers are hung so that they close with a soft click.

Which timber do we use?

Most of our work is in solid oak or ash, with painted poplar for cupboards that will be sprayed. Each board is chosen by hand, and we can show you samples at your visit.

Is there a guarantee?

Every piece carries a ten year guarantee on materials and workmanship. Solid timber moves with the seasons, so read the timber care guide to keep your furniture at its best.

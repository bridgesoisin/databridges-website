Our joinery services

We build kitchens, wardrobes and made-to-measure furniture for homes across Galway and the west of Ireland. Every project starts with a free visit, a measured survey and a drawing you can keep.

What kitchens do you build?

We build shaker, slab and in-frame kitchens in solid timber, with dovetailed drawers and soft-close hinges. Each design is drawn to suit your room rather than adapted from a catalogue, so the layout works for the way you cook.

Do you fit wardrobes as well?

Yes, we make fitted wardrobes, dressing rooms and under-stairs storage to match your kitchen. Doors hang on concealed hinges, and every interior is planned around the clothes, shoes and bags you actually own.

How long does a project take?

Most kitchens take six to eight weeks from signed drawing to final fitting, depending on the timber and finish. We agree the timetable in writing so that you can plan around it with confidence.

Where does your timber come from?

Our oak and ash come from managed Irish and European woodland, supplied through merchants with FSC certification. We turn the offcuts into chopping boards and shelves rather than sending them to landfill.

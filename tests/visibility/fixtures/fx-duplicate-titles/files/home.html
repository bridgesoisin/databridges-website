Bespoke kitchens and fitted wardrobes, made in Galway

Lakeside Joinery is a family workshop at 12 Dock Road, Galway, where we design and build bespoke kitchens and fitted wardrobes from sustainably sourced Irish timber. Every commission is drawn, cut and finished by our own joiners.

What we make

Our kitchens use solid ash, oak and walnut with hand-cut joints and a hard-wearing oil finish. Our wardrobes make full use of awkward alcoves, sloping ceilings and tall Victorian walls.

How a project works

We visit your home, measure carefully and agree a fixed price before any timber is ordered. Most kitchens take between six and eight weeks from the first drawing to the final fitting.

From the workshop journal

Our journal explains the choices behind our work. Start with our guide to choosing oak worktops or read how we season timber before it reaches the bench.

Visit the workshop

Our showroom is open on weekday mornings and by appointment on Saturdays. Bring your room measurements and a few photographs, and we will talk through timber samples, finishes and realistic budgets over a cup of tea.

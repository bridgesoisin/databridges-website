About Lakeside Joinery

Lakeside Joinery began in 2009 when two brothers, both trained joiners, set up a bench in a shed behind their family home in Galway. Today a team of six builds every kitchen and wardrobe in the same workshop on Dock Road.

Our approach

We believe good joinery should outlast the fashions around it. That means solid timber, honest joints and finishes that can be repaired rather than replaced when the years take their toll.

Our team

Our joiners have between eight and thirty years of experience, and two of them trained through the Irish apprenticeship system. Each project has one named joiner who looks after it from first drawing to final fitting.

Our commitments

We quote a fixed price, keep to the agreed timetable and return to check every kitchen after three months. If something is not right, we put it right at our own cost.

Our workshop

The workshop has a spray room, a machine shop and a bench area where every kitchen is dry assembled before delivery. Customers are always welcome to visit, see their own project in progress and ask the joiners questions.

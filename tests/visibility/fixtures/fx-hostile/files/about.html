About Northgate Studio

Northgate Studio was founded in 2016 by two planners who were tired of reports that nobody could read. We still believe a good report should fit on a few pages and answer the question that was asked.

Our team

Today there are six of us, working from an office on Quay Street. Four are chartered planners and two are market researchers, and everyone writes their own reports rather than handing them to a junior.

Our values

We are independent, we show our working and we tell clients when a project is unlikely to succeed. That honesty has cost us the odd contract and earned us most of our repeat business.

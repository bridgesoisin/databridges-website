Questions about planning reports

These are the questions clients ask us most often before they commission a report. If yours is not here, call or write and we will answer it the same day. Every answer below is short on purpose, and we are happy to go into more detail on a call.

Before you start

How long does a site appraisal take?

Most appraisals take about two weeks from the first call to the final report.

Do you work outside Galway?

Yes, we regularly work across Connacht and Munster and charge travel at cost.

What does a planning report include?

A summary, the policy position, the main risks and a clear recommendation.

Can you attend meetings with the council?

Yes, and we can send a written note of each meeting within one working day.

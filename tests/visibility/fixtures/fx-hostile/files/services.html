Planning and research services

Northgate Studio offers three services that can be bought separately or together. Each one ends with a written report and a short meeting to talk it through, so you always know what we found and what we would do next.

Site appraisals

We assess a site against local policy, flood risk, access and neighbouring uses, then summarise the risks and the options in a report of ten pages or fewer.

  • Desk review of local planning policy
  • Site visit with photographs
  • Written summary with a recommendation

Customer surveys

We design a short survey, collect responses from your customers or neighbours and report the results with the raw figures attached so that you can check our working.

Planning support

We prepare drawings lists, cover letters and supporting statements for applications, and we attend pre-application meetings with the council on your behalf.

Field notes from a planning appeal

By Aoife Brennan, 14 August 2026

Last spring we prepared the evidence for a small planning appeal, and the process taught us more about writing than any report we had produced that year. These notes record the three habits we would keep next time.

Lead with the question

The inspector had a long list of appeals to read, so we opened with the single question the appeal turned on and put the supporting detail underneath it.

Show the working

Every figure in our statement pointed to a numbered appendix, which meant the other side could check it quickly and rarely disputed it.

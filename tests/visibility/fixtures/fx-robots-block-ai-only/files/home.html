Bookkeeping and payroll for small Irish businesses

Kestrel Bookkeeping helps owner-managed businesses in Naas and across Kildare keep their books tidy, run payroll on time and file VAT returns without stress. We are a small, friendly practice and we answer our own phone.

What we do

We look after the everyday numbers so that you can look after your customers. Our team provides bookkeeping, payroll and VAT services for sole traders, partnerships and limited companies. We keep everything in cloud software, so you can see your figures whenever you need them. Clients tell us they value plain language, fixed monthly fees and a named person who knows their business.

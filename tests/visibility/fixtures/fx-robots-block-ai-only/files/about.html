About Kestrel Bookkeeping

Meet Aoife Brennan, founder and lead bookkeeper

Kestrel Bookkeeping is a small practice based in Naas. We were founded in 2016 by Aoife Brennan, a bookkeeper who wanted to give owner-managed businesses the same quality of financial information that larger firms take for granted.

Our story

Aoife spent ten years working in finance teams before she opened the practice. She kept meeting business owners who were proud of what they had built but anxious about their paperwork. Kestrel Bookkeeping was set up so that those owners could hand the numbers to someone they trust and get back to their customers. Since then the practice has grown steadily by recommendation, and most of our new clients come to us through people we already work with.

How we work

Every client has one named contact who looks after their books from the first month. We use cloud software, we explain every report in plain language and we agree a fixed fee in advance. You can read more about our services or see how pricing works on the same page.

What we value

We value accuracy, honesty and good manners. We meet deadlines, we tell you early when something looks wrong and we never use jargon to hide a problem. If that sounds like the kind of support your business needs, talk to us about how we can help.

Frequently asked questions about bookkeeping

These are the questions we hear most often from new clients, whether they run a shop, a trade or a consultancy. If your question is not here, please contact us and we will answer it personally.

Every answer below is written in plain language and checked by the person who will look after your account. We update this page whenever Revenue changes a rule or a deadline that affects our clients, so the guidance stays current. You can also ask us about anything that is specific to your trade, such as construction, hospitality or retail.

What does a bookkeeper actually do?

A bookkeeper records your sales, purchases and bank transactions and turns them into reports you can read. That gives you reliable figures for your accountant and for Revenue, and we chase missing receipts so that nothing slips through.

How much does bookkeeping cost for a small business?

Most clients pay a fixed monthly fee based on the number of transactions and employees, quoted in writing after a short call. The fee stays the same until we agree a change together.

Do I need to register for VAT?

You must register once your turnover passes the Revenue threshold for your type of business. If you are close, we will tell you early, explain how registration affects your prices and handle the paperwork for you.

When are payroll submissions due?

Payroll submissions must reach Revenue on or before the date each employee is paid. We build the submission into every pay run, so it is filed on time and you approve the figures first.

Can you work with the software I already use?

Usually, yes, because we work with the main cloud accounting packages and can take over an existing set of books after a short review. If your software is a poor fit, we will suggest a better option.

How do I switch to Kestrel Bookkeeping?

Book an introduction call and we will agree what you need and when we start. We then request your records from your current provider and set up your accounts, and most clients are fully moved over within two weeks.

You can also read about our bookkeeping, payroll and VAT services and about the way we price them. If you would like to talk it through, we offer a free introduction call with no obligation.

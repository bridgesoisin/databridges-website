Bookkeeping, payroll and VAT services

We offer three core services to owner-managed businesses in Naas and across Kildare. Each one is priced as a fixed monthly fee, so you always know what to expect from us.

What we do each month

Each month we record your sales and purchases, reconcile your bank accounts and send you a short summary of where the business stands. You get one named contact who reviews everything before it reaches you.

We work in cloud accounting software so that you can see the same figures that we do. If something looks unusual, we ask before we assume, and we explain the answer in plain language. At the end of each quarter we also review your figures with you and flag anything worth acting on before it becomes a problem.

How our payroll service works

We collect your hours or salary details, calculate pay and deductions, submit the figures to Revenue and send payslips to your team. You approve each run before anything is filed.

  • Weekly, fortnightly or monthly pay runs
  • Payslips issued to each employee by email
  • Revenue submissions filed on time
  • Year-end returns prepared for you

VAT returns and Revenue deadlines

We prepare and file your VAT returns on time.

Revenue guidance changes from time to time, so we check the official VAT registration guidance before every return. If you are close to a registration threshold, we will tell you early and explain what it means for your prices and your paperwork.

Fixed monthly fees

Our fees are set once a year and agreed in writing before we start. Most small businesses pay a single monthly amount that covers bookkeeping, a payroll run for up to five employees and the preparation of every VAT return. There are no hourly charges, no surprise extras and no long contracts, so you can stop whenever you choose with one month's notice. If your business grows or your needs change, we review the fee together and agree any change before it takes effect.

Whatever you choose, you keep full ownership of your records. You can download your data from the accounting software at any time, and we will hand everything over in a tidy format if you ever decide to leave.

Our services

Willow Bakery has been baking for the people of Cork since the shop first opened its doors on a wet Tuesday morning. We keep our range small so that everything on the shelf is fresh. Most of our regular customers call in on their way to work and take home a loaf for the evening.

We also take orders for birthdays, christenings and weddings, and we are always happy to talk through what you have in mind before you commit to anything.

Prices depend on the size of the order and on the ingredients you choose. We will always give you a clear figure in writing before any baking begins, so there are no surprises on the day. Most orders need about three days notice.

Cakes

Our cakes are baked to order from butter, free range eggs and flour milled a few miles from the shop.

Bread

Our bread is shaped by hand each morning and left to rise slowly so that the crust stays crisp.

Catering

We supply sandwiches, pastries and platters for offices, clubs and private parties anywhere in the city, and we deliver them ourselves.

Gift boxes

Our gift boxes hold a loaf, a small cake and a jar of jam, and we will tie them with ribbon on request.

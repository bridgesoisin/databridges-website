Questions we get asked

Do you make gluten-free bread?

Yes, we bake one gluten-free loaf each Friday, and it is made on separate trays so that nothing is shared with the wheat loaves.

Can I order a cake online?

Yes, send us an email at least three days ahead with the date, the size and the flavour you would like, and we will confirm the price.

Where do you deliver?

Within Cork city.

Do you use nuts?

Sometimes. Ask us.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, any card.

Opening times

We open at seven every morning except Sunday.

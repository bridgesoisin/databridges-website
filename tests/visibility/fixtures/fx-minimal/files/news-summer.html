Summer opening hours

The summer is our busiest season at Willow Bakery. The shop fills up with visitors from early morning until the last tray of scones is gone. We have changed our hours a little so that the whole team can keep up with the demand. Nothing about the baking itself will change. The bread will still be fresh each morning and the cakes will still be made to order. Please read the details below before you plan a visit.

Our regular customers know that the best loaves go first. If you want to be sure of a particular loaf or a box of pastries for the office, send us a message the evening before and we will put it aside with your name on it.

When are we open?

From the first of June until the end of August we open early and close later in the day. The shop stays open until the bread has sold out.

Weekdays

Seven until five. Closed for lunch.

Seven until six.

Sundays

Eight until noon.

Eight until two.

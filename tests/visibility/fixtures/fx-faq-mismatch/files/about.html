About Blackwater Accounting

Blackwater Accounting was founded in 2004 by two chartered accountants who wanted to give small businesses the same care that larger firms offer their biggest clients. We still work from the same office on Mill Lane in Fermoy.

Our accountants

Our four accountants are members of Chartered Accountants Ireland and bring experience from farming, retail, construction and hospitality. Most of them grew up within twenty miles of the office.

Our values

We believe tax should be explained, not hidden behind jargon. We tell you what you owe, why you owe it and what you can legitimately do to reduce it.

Our commitments

We agree a fixed fee before we start, we meet every filing date and we never submit a return that you have not seen and understood first.

Our office

Our office on Mill Lane has a meeting room, a small library of tax guides and a kettle that is rarely cold. Clients are welcome to call in without an appointment during the quieter months of the year.

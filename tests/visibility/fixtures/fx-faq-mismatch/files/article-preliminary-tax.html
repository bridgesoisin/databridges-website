Preliminary tax explained for sole traders

By Mairead Cronin, 10 June 2026

Preliminary tax is the advance payment of your income tax for the current year. Many new sole traders are surprised by it, so a short explanation can save an unwelcome shock.

What is preliminary tax?

It is a payment on account of the tax you expect to owe for the year. Revenue asks for it in advance so that the final balance at filing time is smaller.

When is preliminary tax due?

For most self-employed people it is due on the same day as the return for the previous year. Check the exact dates each year on the Revenue website.

How is the amount calculated?

You must pay a set share of the current year liability, or the whole of the previous year, depending on the option you choose. We will work out the safest figure for you.

Can you pay in instalments?

Yes, you can spread payments across the year by direct debit, which helps cash flow. Ask us to set this up early so that the first instalment is not missed.

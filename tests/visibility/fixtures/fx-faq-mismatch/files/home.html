Practical accounting for Fermoy businesses

Blackwater Accounting is a practice at 7 Mill Lane, Fermoy, preparing tax returns and bookkeeping for sole traders, farmers and small companies across north Cork. We keep your records straightforward and your filing dates under control.

What we do

We prepare annual returns, keep monthly books, run payroll and produce company accounts. Every client has one named accountant who knows the business and who answers the phone when you call.

How we work

We start with a short meeting to understand your income, your costs and your plans for the year. After that we agree a fixed fee, so you always know what the service will cost.

Guidance for owners

Our short guides explain tax in plain language. Start with our guide to preliminary tax or read about the basics of VAT registration before you take on your first employee.

Visit the office

Our office is open on weekdays from nine until five, with evening appointments by arrangement during the busy filing season. There is parking on Mill Lane and the ground floor is fully accessible.

Frequently asked questions

Here are the questions clients ask us most often before they decide to work with us. If you cannot find your answer, please phone the office and we will explain.

Do you work with sole traders?

Yes, sole traders and small partnerships make up most of our clients. We prepare your annual return, keep your records in order and remind you of every filing date well in advance.

Can you help me register for VAT?

We can check whether your turnover requires registration, complete the application and set up your first return. Our guidance follows the rules published by Revenue.

What should I bring to our first meeting?

Bring your tax reference number, last year's return if you have one and any receipts or bank statements for the current year. We will tell you what else is needed once we have talked.

How quickly can you file my return?

Once we have all of your records, most returns are filed within five working days. Complex returns with several income sources can take a little longer, and we will tell you in advance.

Accounting services

We look after the tax and accounts of sole traders, farmers and small companies across Fermoy and north Cork. Each client has one named accountant who knows the business and answers the phone.

Do you prepare company accounts?

Yes, we prepare annual accounts, file them with the Companies Registration Office and handle the corporation tax return. You receive a clear summary of profit, tax due and cash position each year.

Can you run payroll for my staff?

We run weekly or monthly payroll, submit every pay run to Revenue and issue payslips by email. We also look after pension deductions and year-end returns for each employee.

What does bookkeeping cost?

Bookkeeping is priced by the number of transactions each month, with a fixed fee agreed in writing. Most small businesses pay between sixty and one hundred and fifty euro monthly.

How often will we meet?

We meet at least twice a year to review your figures and plan ahead. Between meetings you can call or email us at any time and we will reply within one working day.

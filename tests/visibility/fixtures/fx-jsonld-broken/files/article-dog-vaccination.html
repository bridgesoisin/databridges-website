Dog vaccination schedule explained

By Ciara Walsh, 2 July 2026

Vaccination protects dogs from several serious infections that still circulate in Ireland. The right schedule depends on the age of your dog, its lifestyle and any earlier doses.

Why do puppies need several vaccinations?

Antibodies from a puppy's mother fade at different rates, so a single early dose can be blocked. A short course of injections makes sure the puppy is protected once those antibodies have gone.

When should the first dose be given?

The first dose is usually given at around eight weeks, with the second two to four weeks later. We will confirm the dates for your puppy at the first visit and book the follow-up for you.

How often are boosters needed?

Most dogs need a booster once a year, although some components can be given every three years. Your vet will explain the options and the Veterinary Council of Ireland publishes further guidance.

Are there any side effects to watch for?

Mild tiredness or a small lump at the injection site is normal for a day or two. Call the clinic if your dog vomits, swells around the face or seems very unwell.

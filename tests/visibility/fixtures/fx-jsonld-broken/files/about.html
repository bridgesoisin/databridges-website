About Corrib Veterinary Clinic

Corrib Veterinary Clinic opened in 2011 in a converted shop on Main Street, Oughterard. It is still owned by the two vets who founded it, and many of our patients belong to second or third generation clients.

Our vets

Both of our vets trained in Dublin and worked in busy city practices before moving west. Between them they have more than thirty years of experience with dogs, cats and small mammals.

Our nurses

Three registered veterinary nurses run our weight clinics, dental checks and puppy classes. They are often the people who spot a small change in an animal that an owner has not yet noticed.

Our promise

We explain every option in plain language, give you a written estimate before treatment and never recommend a procedure that your pet does not need.

Our clinic

The clinic has two consulting rooms, a small operating theatre and a separate quiet room for cats. We also keep a dedicated kennel area so that animals recovering from surgery are never left in a busy corridor.

Friendly small animal care in Oughterard

Corrib Veterinary Clinic is a small animal practice at 4 Main Street, Oughterard, offering small animal medicine, pet vaccinations and dental care for dogs, cats and rabbits. Our two vets and three nurses have looked after local pets since 2011.

What we offer

We run daily consultations, routine vaccinations, microchipping, dental scaling and minor surgery. Our consulting rooms are arranged so that nervous animals can be seen quietly, without waiting beside other patients.

When we are open

The clinic is open from nine until six on weekdays and from nine until one on Saturdays. Outside those hours our emergency line puts you through to a vet on call.

Pet care advice

Our short guides cover the questions owners ask most often. Start with our dog vaccination schedule or read about spring parasite checks before the weather warms up.

Find us

We are on the main street in Oughterard, with free parking behind the building and step-free access from the car park. Please call ahead if you are bringing a large dog or several animals.

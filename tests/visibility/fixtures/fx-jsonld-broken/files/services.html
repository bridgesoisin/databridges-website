Our veterinary services

We treat dogs, cats, rabbits and other small pets at our clinic on the main street in Oughterard. Every visit begins with a calm, unhurried examination and a clear explanation of what we find.

Do you offer routine vaccinations?

Yes, we vaccinate puppies, kittens, adult dogs and cats, and we keep a reminder list so that boosters are never missed. Rabbits can be protected against the two main diseases at the same appointment.

Can you carry out dental treatment?

We scale and polish teeth under anaesthetic and remove any that are beyond saving. Dental checks are included in every annual health examination, so problems are caught while they are still small.

What happens at a first appointment?

We weigh your pet, check heart, lungs, teeth and skin, and talk through diet and parasite control. All of our vets are registered with the Veterinary Council of Ireland.

How do I book an emergency visit?

Phone the clinic number at any hour and follow the options for emergencies. A vet on call will advise you over the phone and meet you at the clinic if your pet needs to be seen.

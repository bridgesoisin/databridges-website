Pet care questions answered

These are the questions our reception team hears most often. If your question is not covered here, please phone the clinic and we will be glad to help.

Do you treat rabbits and other small pets?

Yes, we see rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters and ferrets. Please tell us when you book so that we can allow enough time and keep the waiting area calm for your pet.

Can I bring my pet without an appointment?

We keep a short walk-in session every weekday morning for urgent but non-emergency problems. Outside that session an appointment is best, because it means a vet is free to see your pet straight away.

What should I do if my pet eats something unsafe?

Phone us straight away, even if your pet seems well, and tell us what was eaten and roughly when. Please do not try to make your pet vomit unless a vet has told you to.

How much does a consultation cost?

Our current fees are displayed in the waiting room and given to you in writing before any treatment. The Veterinary Council of Ireland publishes guidance on how vets should explain their charges.
